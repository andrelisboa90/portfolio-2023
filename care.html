You Care

Mobile app Admin web Logo design

• 2026

You Care is a third-party caregiving app that allows users to log a person's daily care routines. These care activities are displayed as a feed on the home screen, providing a complete history of all the care received.

Through the app, caregivers can manage care for individuals with special needs and export detailed PDF reports to share.

User Personas

Anna Williams, 42

Licensed professional caregiver

Anna works independently, supporting multiple elderly clients. She needs to document care clearly, consistently, and professionally to maintain accurate records, support medical reviews, and protect herself legally.

John Thompson, 29

Social work assistant (volunteer)

John supports vulnerable adults through a community program. He needs to record objective observations while respecting privacy and working in environments with limited connectivity.

High fidelities

Authentication

Users log in with their email and password to access the care feed directly. Alternatively, they can sign up by providing their name, email, and password.

Care feed

Users can check the care feed right from the app's home screen. Additionally, they can view detailed information about the care recipient.

Log care

Users can log care directly from the bottom navigation bar. They are presented with various care categories, each featuring tailored fields to complete.

Dark mode

The app also offers a dark mode option, allowing users to choose the theme that best fits their preference.
