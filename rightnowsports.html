Right Now Sports

 • Mobile App / Admin Web  • 2026

Right Now Sports is a mobile social network for sports enthusiasts. On the platform, users can create sports battles with other users, view nearby events on a map, and post reels and live streams of themselves practicing their favorite sports.

My role spanned from initial product ideation—including persona research, sitemaps, and wireframes—to visual design, delivering high-fidelity screens, branding, and marketing assets.

User personas

Jordan, 24

The Brand Owner Seeking Athlete Partnerships

Jordan wants visibility among active sports communities and prefers partnering with real players instead of influencers disconnected from the sport. Right Now Sports provides a space to observe athletes before deciding who to support or sponsor.

Riley, 22

The Social Fan and Content Viewer

Riley enjoys sports culture and competition but mainly participates as a viewer and community member rather than a competitor.

Alexis, 27

The Competitive Player Building Reputation

Alexis is a serious soccer player who wants recognition, competition, and ranking progression through live Battles.

Wireframes

High fidelities

Authentication

Authentication consists of the login flow or the new user signup flow. To create an account, simply provide your personal information and profile preferences, such as favorite sports, bio, and profile picture.

Events

Users can explore events across different sports in a map view and RSVP to confirm their attendance. Additionally, they can create their own events.

Battles

Users can set up battles with other players directly within the app. Battles can be filmed by multiple cameras simultaneously, and third-party voting is enabled to decide the winner.

Admin web

Through the web admin dashboard, moderators can perform platform actions. It enables them to manage users, moderate content, access audit logs, and analyze app usage analytics.
